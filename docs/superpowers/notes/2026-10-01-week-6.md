# Week 6 — historical sprint-report evidence

Implemented changelog-backed historical metrics for closed sprint reports.
`reports/history.py` reconstructs initial commitment, additions/removals,
start-time estimates and their changes, completion during the sprint, reopened
work and evidence-backed rollover from ordered `issue_events`. Current
snapshots only provide issue context; they do not fill historical gaps.

The report preserves the `adr-0002/changelog-v1` policy version and evidence
in its durable JSON job result. Missing sprint boundaries, missing issue
history or same-timestamp membership ordering produce an explicit
`unavailable` or `partial` result with named evidence gaps. The UI and exports
show the historical status and metrics. Subtasks are excluded by default.

Fixture verification covers a reopened/rolled-over issue, a post-start
estimate (unknown at commitment), timezone-aware boundaries, missing
boundaries and subtask exclusion. A manually reviewed real closed sprint and
same-board velocity trend remain open until a sufficiently observed sprint
closes.
